SOLMAR BAY

An original sun-soaked coastal crime city. No Rockstar, no GTA — this bay is ours.

You are new in town. Carmen "La Jefa" Duarte runs the bay, Los Tiburones want it back, and Solmar PD wants you both. Drive, steal, fight and run 8 missions to become King of the Bay.

    WASD move/drive • Mouse look • Shift run • Space jump/handbrake • E enter car / shop • F punch • Click shoot • 1/2/3 weapons • R reload • P pause

    HOW TO PLAY

    On foot

    WASD walk, Shift run, Space jump. Mouse looks around (click to lock). F punches, click shoots when armed, 1/2/3 switch weapons, R reloads.

    Driving

    E near a car to get in — an occupied car is a steal (+1 star). WASD drive, Space handbrake, E to get out. Watch the speedometer.

    Wanted

    Stars rise for stealing, hitting pedestrians and shooting near police. They decay after ~20 s clean. Pay'n'Spray clears them for $150.

    Money & health

    Missions pay $200–$5,000. Spend at GUN RUNNER (ammo), Diner Sol (heal), Hospital (full heal) and Pay'n'Spray. Health regens out of combat.

    Missions

    Follow the gold beacon and the minimap. 8 missions, from a pizza run to the Harbor Heist and the finale at Tiburones HQ.

    Busted / Wasted

    Arrested or killed = game over with a $100 penalty. Progress (cash, missions) saves to this browser — CONTINUE picks it up.
